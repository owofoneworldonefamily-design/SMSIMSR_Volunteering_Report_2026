Core Volunteer Team

Other activities
in the hospital

Apart from their regular duties, the core team also takes part in various activities in the hospital, such as cleanliness counselling for patients and their attendants. They teach them how to use the toilets and bathrooms and how to keep the ward clean.

Gratitude Awareness Sessions

The team also provides gratitude counselling to patients, helping them recognise and appreciate the various opportunities available to them. This initiative has been ongoing for some time, and the team is now undergoing further training under Dr. Padma Sree.

Village visits

Some of the parent volunteers invited the coordinators to their villages. There were 4 village visits. They gathered other parents and also patients who had received treatment at the hospital.

In their own words · 1 of 3

Manjula

Parent volunteer
“

During our initial seva, my nephew suddenly developed severe appendicitis right before our term ended. The hospital responded immediately, providing swift emergency care and performing a successful surgery within the week. Thanks to the staff’s prompt support and Swami's grace, he recovered completely and now eagerly looks forward to serving again.

Swami does not demand massive donations; He only asks for our selfless service, our sincere understanding of this institution, and our voice to spread awareness. Contributing our humble squirrel's share (alilu seva) is a meaningful responsibility we can each embrace.

Our true strength lies in collective effort. Instead of leaving the burden to a single person, we must unite as a hundred supporting hands, walking behind Swami to carry His mission forward together.

Geetha

Parent volunteer
“

I tell many people in my town about this institution and have sent patients here for consultations and hand surgeries. A boy in our family also got a school seat at the Mysuru campus.

Because I work at Manipal University College, my biggest wish is to ask our General Manager for some help from our university for the ashram and the hospital. I have the courage to make this request, and I strongly believe Swami will stand by me and help make it happen.

To me, Sri Sathya Sai Grama in Muddenahalli is nothing short of the world's eighth wonder, and I pray my five-year bond with it endures for a lifetime. Serving here brings me profound joy. Leaving home for seva fills me with excitement, while departing feels like a daughter reluctantly leaving her maternal home. Though financial obligations currently prevent me from dedicating myself here full-time, my heart remains anchored in lifelong service.

Driven by this devotion, I actively spread word of the mission and reach out to prospective supporters online, praying my appeals inspire others to contribute.

Shashikala

Parent volunteer
“

Shashikala from Kolar shares that when her family faced severe financial hardship, Swami uplifted them by admitting her elder daughter to the Jayapura campus for class 6, followed by her younger daughter (now in 9th and 6th grades). Grateful for this support, her entire family surrendered to Swami.

For last 5 years she has been doing Seva and experiencing the joy of volunteering.

Highlighting Swami’s guidance that the mission requires both people and funds (Jana and Dhana), she emphasizes that devotees earning outside should work to raise funds and support the mission in whatever way they can.

She says that each one associated with the mission must act as ambassadors, taking responsibility to spread awareness about the humanitarian mission and follow whatever path Swami guides them on.
